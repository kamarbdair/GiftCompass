GiftCompass builds a picture of the person you're buying for, then recommends gifts that fit them, the occasion and your budget, with a reason for each one.

Two ways to describe the recipient

AAvailable now

Answer 10 quick questions about them

Tell us your relationship, the occasion, their interests and personality, the kind of gift they'd like, what they've said they want, what they already have, what to avoid, and your budget. It takes about 2 minutes.

BComing later

Recipient-authorised social media profile

In a future version, the recipient themselves could choose to share interest signals from their own social media account (for example TikTok) through the platform's official login. They give explicit permission and can withdraw it at any time.
