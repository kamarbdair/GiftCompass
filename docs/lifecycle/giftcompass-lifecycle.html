Steps 4–6 up close: how consent works

Participation is invited, never assumed — recommendations happen either way.

GiftCompass  Creates a secure invitation link, which the sender passes to the recipient
Recipient decides  Take part? Consent is explicit and voluntary
Yes — shares interestsConnect TikTok (official authorised access), or answer a short questionnaire
No — declines or never repliesNothing is collected from the recipient
GiftCompass builds the recipient interest profilefrom the recipient's answers — or, if there are none, from what the sender provided

Privacy & trust, built in

The recipient is a person, not a data source.

✓
Consent is asked for first, and is explicit and voluntary
✓
No scraping, no private messages, no hidden collection
✓
Social media only through official, authorised access
✓
Only what is needed to match a gift
✓
The recipient reviews every inferred interest and can remove any of them
✓
Consent can be withdrawn, and the saved profile deleted, at any time

In plain
words

Sarah wants a birthday gift for her friend. She creates a GiftCompass account, sends her friend an optional preference invitation, and gets personalised recommendations with a reason for each. She buys one and the retailer delivers it. Next occasion, Sarah reuses her friend's saved profile — she enters only the new occasion and budget, and GiftCompass suggests something new.
